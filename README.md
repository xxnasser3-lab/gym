# Gym Planner

A calm, single-file gym app. It plans A/B/C/D workouts around your shifts, suggests weights, and sets protein, carbs and fat from your weight.

Open the site on your phone and add it to the Home Screen. Data stays on the device, so use Settings > Backup now and then.

Files: `index.html` is the whole app, `sw.js` makes it work offline, `manifest.webmanifest` and `icons/` make it installable.

Credits: muscle map outlines come from react-body-highlighter (MIT). Exercise photos in `ex/` come from free-exercise-db (public domain, Unlicense).
